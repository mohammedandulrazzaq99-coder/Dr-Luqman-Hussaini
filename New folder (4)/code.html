THE JOURNEY IN DETAIL

A closer look at the key stages.

2004 01

Undergraduate Studies

MBA, Osmania University

Completed MBA at Osmania University in 2004, which provided a strong foundation for his interest in organisational development and people-related work.

2015 02

Postgraduate Study

MA (Psychology), Indira Gandhi National Open University

Completed MA in Psychology in 2015, deepening his understanding of human behaviour, learning and development.

2015 03

Doctoral Research

PhD, Dr. Babasaheb Ambedkar Marathwada University

Completed PhD in 2015, with research focused on employee retention and related themes, contributing to the understanding of people management in organisational contexts.

Academic Years 04

Teaching & Academic Work

Various institutions

Gained extensive teaching experience at undergraduate, graduate and postgraduate levels, working with students across different disciplines.

Leadership Role 05

Director / Principal

Gyanam Academy / Gyanam Junior College

Currently serving as Director / Principal, contributing to institutional leadership, academic planning and the development of learning environments.

Ongoing 06

Training, Research & Public Engagement

Education, leadership and human development

Continues to be engaged in teaching, training, research, writing and public engagement on themes related to education, leadership, psychology and human development.

FROM JOURNEY TO IMPACT

Each stage of the journey has added depth, perspective and practical understanding. This ongoing experience informs his current work in education, leadership, psychology and human resource development, and his commitment to enabling better individuals and stronger institutions.
